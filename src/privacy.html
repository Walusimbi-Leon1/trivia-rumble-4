Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Trivia Rumble Elite is a Discord Activity game. When you play inside Discord, we use Discord's embedded app SDK to show you the game, and we receive:

  • Your Discord user ID, username, and avatar (to display your score and profile in the game)
  • The channel ID of the voice channel where the activity is running (used as the game room)

Game state (rooms, players, scores, questions) is stored in a Firebase Realtime Database. No messages or channel content are read or stored. We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is retained only as long as a game room exists and is deleted when the room is empty.

Questions may be generated by an AI service (Groq) when enabled; otherwise a built-in question bank is used. AI-generated content is not personalized.
